---
name: No-show customer warning
description: Product rule for the informational warning shown before a client books after a recorded no-show.
---

After a barber records a no-show, the customer's next public booking may show a prominent 10-second warning, but the warning itself must never block or modify the customer's account.

**Why:** The user confirmed the warning works and explicitly required it to remain informational, with no account blocking or alteration.

**How to apply:** Keep this customer-facing warning separate from the barber's no-show action and loyalty-point rules. Do not add automatic account sanctions to the warning flow.
